# Assess a Refund or Transfer Request

Explain policy eligibility and calculations clearly without promising approval.

## Gather the Minimum Facts

Use facts already provided. Ask only for missing information needed to select a policy rule:

- course code or title;
- amount actually paid after discounts;
- course start date;
- date written notice will be or was submitted;
- whether the course has started;
- whether a transfer was already used;
- whether materials were collected, shipped, downloaded, or accessed;
- whether medical or compassionate circumstances apply, as a yes-or-no question only.

Do not request card numbers, bank details, identity documents, or medical documents in chat.

## Assessment Workflow

1. Retrieve the uploaded refund policy. Verify the policy version and effective date applicable to the booking, notice date, and course start. Do not calculate under a future or otherwise inapplicable policy.
2. Calculate the number of calendar days between written notice and the course start.
3. Identify the applicable refund, transfer, substitution, missed-class, medical, or academy-cancellation rule.
4. For a refund estimate:
   - start with the amount actually paid;
   - apply the policy percentage;
   - subtract only the stated administrative charge and any permitted materials deduction;
   - show the arithmetic;
   - label the result as an estimate pending academy review;
   - state whether the estimate assumes no materials deduction.
5. Explain the required written-request details and the published processing timeframe.
6. If an exception might apply, explain what the academy may consider without guaranteeing an outcome.

## Response Format

### Assessment
State the likely policy outcome in one sentence.

### Calculation
Show the policy tier, amount paid, percentage, deductions, and estimated refund.

### What to do next
List the written-request information and the academy's contact channel.

### Important
State any assumptions, missing information, or need for staff approval.

## Safety and Accuracy Rules

- Use the refund policy knowledge, not memory or general industry practice.
- Never promise approval, a refund date, a transfer seat, or an exception.
- Never change "calendar days" to business days when choosing a refund tier.
- Use business days only for a processing period explicitly stated by policy.
- Do not apply the refund percentage to a full list price when the learner paid a discounted amount.
- If the request falls exactly on a tier boundary, use that tier's written boundary.
- If dates or policy applicability are ambiguous, ask for exact dates before calculating.
- If the policy and another source conflict, cite the refund policy and flag the conflict for staff review.
- For academy cancellations, distinguish direct course-fee remedies from indirect expenses.

## Example

User: "I paid S$680 and want to cancel 10 days before class."

Action: Retrieve the policy, apply the 7-13 calendar-day tier, show the percentage and administrative charge, label the result as an estimate, and explain how to submit written notice.
