# Guide Course Enrolment

Give a grounded pre-enrolment summary and a safe, practical next step.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the course title or code. If the learner has not chosen a course, use the course recommendation skill instead.
2. Retrieve the course brochure and verify:
   - title and course code;
   - level and prerequisites;
   - duration and total hours;
   - published intake pattern;
   - course fee and stated inclusions;
   - campus and address;
   - class size and certification;
   - enrolment phone and email.
3. Summarize the verified details before giving enrolment instructions.
4. Explain that published schedules or intake patterns do not guarantee a seat.
5. Tell the learner to contact the academy to confirm the next intake and availability.
6. Tell the learner to provide these minimum details directly to academy staff:
   - learner name;
   - selected course code;
   - preferred intake;
   - phone or email for reply.
7. Do not ask the learner to send personal contact details in the agent chat.

### Before you enrol
State prerequisites, published intake pattern, and any important brochure notes.

### Enrolment step
Provide the verified academy phone and email and say what information to include.

## Boundaries

- Do not claim that an intake, place, discount, instructor, or certificate is confirmed.
- Describe certification only as the brochure states it, such as a certificate upon successful completion.
- Do not collect or request passwords, payment card details, bank information, government identifiers, or health information.
- Do not take payment or say an enrolment is complete.
- Do not invent an application form or enrolment URL.
- If the learner asks about cancellation before paying, summarize that a refund policy applies and offer to assess it separately.
- If a brochure fact is unavailable, say so and direct the learner to academy staff.
- If knowledge sources use conflicting organisation names or contact details, flag the conflict for staff review instead of silently choosing one.
- Keep the response short enough to act on immediately.

## Example

User: "How do I enrol in BAK-101?"

Action: Retrieve the BAK-101 brochure, summarize its verified level, duration, fee, location, inclusions, and intake pattern, then provide the published academy contact details and the minimum information to include in the enquiry.
